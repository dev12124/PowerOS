## ⚖️ Core Philosophy: Public Domain Commitment
The PowerOS is established entirely within the **Public Domain**. The foundational code-base is un-owned, freely shared with humanity, and carries zero traditional copyleft enforcement. 

To maintain the project's structural integrity, security standards, and high-performance bare-metal execution parameters, this repository operates under a **Founder-Led Consensus** model.

---

## 🏛️ Decision Making Process: Founder-Led Consensus
While the code itself is free of domain constraints, updates merged into the **official QMX Corporation repository** are managed via a transparent technical pipeline:

1. **The Role of the Founder (QMX Lead):** As the founder and original architect of the system, **João Guilherme da Silva Freitas Lima** operates as the head technical coordinator. While not an enforcement owner due to public domain parameters, the founder retains final merge validation rights for the master branch layout.
2. **Community Consensus:** Developers are free to fork, mutate, and rebuild PowerOS variants independently. Contributions targeted for the core implementation should align with the rules established in `.clang-format` and `.editorconfig`.
3. **The Final Gate:** Pull Requests submitted to this specific core repository undergo evaluation based strictly on hardware efficiency, binary size optimization, and compilation safety.
